# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: January 13, 2026**

## Overview

Vibe Pulse is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our approach to data collection and storage.

## Data Collection

**We do not collect any personal information or usage data.**

Vibe Pulse operates entirely on your local device and does not:
- Collect any personal information
- Track your usage or behavior
- Send any data to external servers
- Use analytics or tracking services
- Share any information with third parties

## Data Storage

**All data is stored locally on your device.**

- All application data, settings, and content remain exclusively on your local device
- You have full control over your data at all times
- No data is transmitted over the internet
- No cloud services or remote servers are used

## Third-Party Services

Vibe Pulse does not integrate with or use any third-party services that would collect or process your data.

## Changes to This Policy

If we make any changes to this Privacy Policy in the future, we will update this document and the "Last updated" date above. Any changes will maintain our commitment to local-only data storage and zero data collection.

## Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our project repository.

---

**In summary:** Your data stays on your device. We don't collect anything. Period.
